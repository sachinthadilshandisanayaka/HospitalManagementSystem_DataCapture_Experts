==================================================
MANDATORY BUSINESS RULES
==================================================

1. Prevent overlapping appointments:

(newStart < existingEnd) && (newEnd > existingStart)

For same practitioner and BOOKED appointments.

2. Cancel only if BOOKED.

3. endTime must be after startTime.
